ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is FootIQ?

FootIQ is an AI-powered football analytics platform that combines historical match data, machine learning, team performance indicators, Elo ratings, SQL analytics and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to explore football matches and generate intelligent insights.
